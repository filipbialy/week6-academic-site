TEACHING

I teach at the meeting point of technology, business and ethics, for students with and without a technical background.

Approach

I focus on architecture before tools. Languages and frameworks change quickly, and AI assistants can now write much of the code. What lasts is understanding how systems fit together and being able to judge whether a solution is a good one.

I also encourage students to keep doing their own thinking. AI tools can support learning, but they should not replace it.

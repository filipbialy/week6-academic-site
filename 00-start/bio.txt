FILIP BIAŁY

Lecturer at the Open Institute of Technology (OPIT), teaching web development, ICT and the ethics of artificial intelligence.

About me

I work where computer science meets political theory. I started programming as a child and worked as a professional web developer as a teenager. For more than twenty years I have worked in ICT, first as a web developer and more recently as an AI ethics consultant.

For the past decade I have researched the politics of digital technologies. My current work focuses on digital capitalism, AI literacy and data governance.

I have held visiting fellowships at the London School of Economics, the University of Cambridge and the Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society in Berlin. I have researched and taught in the United Kingdom, Poland, Germany, Malta and China, and I regularly comment on technology for the media.
